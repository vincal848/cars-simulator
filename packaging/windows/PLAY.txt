C.A.R.S. 0.24 - Combat Arms Region Simulator

Extract the ENTIRE CARS folder, then double-click CARS.exe.
Keep _internal beside the executable. No Python installation is needed.
Windows x64. Offline play. This independent prototype is unsigned.

Start with Guided Tutorial, or Start Campaign to pick your own nation.
F1: CARSapedia / G: strategy graph / R: replay studio / H: hide tutorial
F11 or Alt+Enter: fullscreen windowed / Esc: close panel / Space: end turn
Right-click a province to inspect, develop or recruit. Left-drag pans the map.
F10: sound, preferences and the music collection.

Replays: drag examples/opening.json onto CARS.exe to watch a sample.
The replay studio records this session from faction selection or the last load.
Export before quitting; each export replaces replays/latest.json, so copy it
to keep it. Replays play back only on the ruleset they were recorded with (0.23).

Saves:    %LOCALAPPDATA%\CARS\saves
Replays:  %LOCALAPPDATA%\CARS\replays
Settings: %LOCALAPPDATA%\CARS\settings.json
Replacing or moving the game folder never touches your saves.
If the game ever closes unexpectedly, the details are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\CARS\crash.log.
